# Captions

Captions are reusable text snippets for consistent, fast replies. They are especially useful for product descriptions, campaign copy, and message structures you reuse across Wabot.

## Create a Caption

1. Open **Settings → Caption**.
2. Click **New Caption**.
3. Enter a clear internal **Title**.
4. Write the reusable **Content**.
5. Check the live WhatsApp preview.
6. Click **Create Caption**.

Captions support WhatsApp-style formatting:

- `*bold*`
- `_italic_`
- `~strikethrough~`
- `` `monospace` ``

## Reuse and Maintain Captions

1. Search the caption list by title or content.
2. Use **Copy** to place a caption on your clipboard.
3. Use **Preview** to check its appearance.
4. Use **Edit** to correct or refresh copy.
5. Select multiple captions only when you are ready to delete obsolete content.

:::tip
Use a naming convention such as `Sales – Product A`, `Support – Delivery ETA`, or `Campaign – Raya 2026`. It makes search and reuse much faster for the whole team.
:::
